01 — M87*

Visualizing gravity beyond the textbook case.

I modeled the gravitational field around M87* and used the results to explore how gravity changes with position, how a gravity well communicates that change, and what escape velocity means in the same system.

02 — Flutter

Making coupled motion legible.

The aeroelastic work used two-degree-of-freedom models to study the interaction between structural motion and aerodynamic loading. I translated mass, stiffness, and damping behavior into time responses and stability insight for both aluminum 6061 and steel cases.

03 — Communication

Good computation should help someone else see the idea.

The final step was presentation: choosing plots that make the model’s behavior understandable, explaining the assumptions, and showing where the mathematics becomes useful engineering evidence.
